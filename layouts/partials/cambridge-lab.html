TOEFL Listen & Repeat · 7 sentences

Listen once with the sentence hidden. Repeat aloud, then reveal the text and check missing words, endings and meaning. This uses your browser's synthetic voice, not an exam recording; pronunciation is self-reviewed. 音声合成（文字から作る音声）で保持と再現を練習します。声は録音・送信されず、発音の自動採点はしません。
